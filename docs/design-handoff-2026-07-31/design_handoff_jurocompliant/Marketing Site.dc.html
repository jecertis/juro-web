How it works

Four steps from a URL to evidence a regulator will accept.

avg. runtime 94s
0 credentials required
01

Point at the surface

Submit a domain. We enumerate public assets, subprocessors, transfer paths and consent flows from the outside in.

02

Scan non-custodially

Rules execute in an ephemeral enclave. Nothing is stored, nothing is proxied through us, no agent is installed.

03

Map to obligations

Every finding cites the article it fails — GDPR, DORA or DPDP — with remediation written for the team that owns it.

04

Seal the result

The report is hashed and signed. Anyone holding the bundle can verify it offline, years later, without trusting us.

The stakes

An unproven control is an unmitigated fine.

Supervisory authorities no longer accept intent as a defence. They ask when a control was verified, by whom, and against what evidence. If the answer is a spreadsheet, the answer is a penalty.

Verifiable evidence

Signed at the moment of scan. Verifiable without us.

Each report ships as a bundle: findings, rule versions, timestamps and a detached signature over the canonical hash. Verification is a single offline command against our published key — no portal, no account, no dependency on Jurocompliant existing.

Who we are

Evidence over dashboards

A pretty score does not survive a supervisory request. A signed bundle does. We optimise for the second thing.
