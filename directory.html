Frequently asked questions

Does Juro vet, rank, or endorse the firms in this directory?

No. This directory is a listing of who practices DPDP, GDPR, and DORA compliance work in India: DPO consultants, CA/audit firms, and RegTech vendors. Juro does not verify, score, rank, or certify any firm's work. Inclusion means a firm asked to be listed and described its own practice, nothing more.

Why is the directory empty right now?

The directory is launching in a pilot phase. It opened for firms to request a listing before any listings were published, so no names appear yet. Firms that submit the listing request above will be contacted about being added when the directory goes live.

Is this the same thing as Juro's compliance scanner?

No. Juro's VPC-agent scans at jurocompliant.com produce signed, deterministic evidence from a company's own cloud posture; the public-surface scan is an unsigned starting point. This directory is a separate, unrelated listing of third-party firms that do DPO, audit, and RegTech work. Juro is not a party to any engagement between a visitor and a listed firm.

What happens after a firm submits the 'Get listed' form?

Someone from Juro follows up by email to confirm the details and discuss the directory launch timeline. The form asks for a few self-reported details, including registration status, years practicing, focus area, and a LinkedIn profile, none of which is independently verified at this stage. Submitting the form does not create a listing automatically, and nothing about pricing or placement is decided on this page.
