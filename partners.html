For advisory, attestation & DPO firms

You issue the opinion. Juro supplies the evidence trail underneath it.

Juro scans a client's public surface and cloud posture and produces signed, deterministic DPDP, GDPR, and DORA findings, evidence any party can independently re-verify. Your firm reviews it, applies judgment, and issues the attestation, audit opinion, or advisory recommendation. Juro does not issue opinions.

ⓘ Not to be confused with juro.com, an unrelated contract lifecycle management company. This page is about JeCertis's compliance scanner at jurocompliant.com. See the full disambiguation if you landed here looking for contract software.

Who this is for

Built for firms whose recurring client deliverable is a compliance opinion, readiness report, or ongoing advisory service, not a security scan on its own. Two archetypes fit today:

Attestation & audit firms

Compliance readiness assessments, control audits, and DPDP audits. You issue the opinion; Juro's signed artefacts are the evidence trail underneath it.

DPO & privacy advisory boutiques

Fractional-DPO, DPDP readiness, privacy program advisory. You advise; Juro tools the advice and backs it with a re-verifiable artefact.

Best fit for boutique-to-midsize practices (roughly 5–200 people) serving Indian SaaS, fintech, or healthtech companies with DPDP, GDPR, or DORA exposure.

What the evidence layer adds to your engagement

  • Signed, deterministic findings. Every scan produces a cryptographically signed output tied to a target, a ruleset hash, and a timestamp. Re-run it later against the same snapshot and the hashes match, so the finding is reproducible, not a one-off claim.
  • Independently re-verifiable. Any auditor, regulator, or counterparty can check a finding without taking your word, or Juro's, for it. That is what turns a report into evidence a client's board or an external reviewer can rely on.
  • Cites the regulatory source. Each finding references the specific DPDP section, GDPR article, or DORA clause that fired, so your write-up can point to the same source your client's legal team would check.
  • Non-custodial by design. Deep scans run inside the client's own cloud account under a read-only role. No infrastructure data leaves the client's perimeter, a detail that matters when you are the one vouching for the engagement.
What this is not

Juro does not certify compliance and does not tell your client whether they "pass." It produces findings: what was detected against a rule, at a point in time. The opinion, the readiness call, and the client relationship stay yours. That division of labour is the point.

Frequently asked questions

Is Juro a compliance certification I can issue to clients?

No. Juro produces signed, deterministic evidence of what a client's public surface and cloud posture show against DPDP, GDPR, and DORA rules. It does not certify compliance or issue an opinion. Your firm reviews the findings and issues the attestation, audit opinion, or advisory recommendation. Juro supplies the evidence underneath it.

Is this the same page as the free scanner at jurocompliant.com?

No. The free surface scanner is self-serve, for a company checking its own domain. This page is for advisory, attestation, and DPO firms who want to use Juro's scanning and signed-artefact evidence inside their own client engagements, a different use case with a different setup.

Is Juro (jurocompliant.com) the same company as juro.com?

No. JeCertis (trading as Juro at jurocompliant.com) builds compliance scanning software. juro.com is a separate, unrelated company that makes contract lifecycle management software.
